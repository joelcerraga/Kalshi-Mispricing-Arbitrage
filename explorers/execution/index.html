When a Basket Fails to Fill

A complete basket can have a positive conditional payoff while partial fills remain risky. This analytical laboratory separates expected payoff from the probability of losing money.

How to read it

Change the gross basket discount and the number of legs. The surface maps the probability of loss across discount and per-leg fill probability. The companion graph plots expected payoff and loss probability for the selected discount. Hover for precise scenario values.

Interpretation and limits

The model assumes equally likely outcomes, an exhaustive single-winner partition and independent fills. The fee coefficient is an illustrative raw 7% quadratic schedule. Settlement exceptions, queue priority, correlated fills, funding and later price moves are excluded. This is not an estimated trading return.
